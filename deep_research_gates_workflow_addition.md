## 🔬 MANDATORY DUAL-GATE DEEP RESEARCH & COMPETITOR BENCHMARKING PROTOCOL

To ensure world-class market dominance, zero missing features, and maximum agentic capability, every application MUST execute two mandatory Deep Research Gates:

---

### 🔬 DEEP RESEARCH GATE 1: User Pain Points, Connectors & Market Benchmark (Between Stage 2 & Stage 3)

**Lead Autonomous Agents**: `SEC-01` & `ARCH-01` (Research & Logic Specialists powered by **DeepSeek-R1 / V3**).  
**Execution Timing**: Executed strictly between Stage 2 (Solution Architecture) and Stage 3 (UX/UI Design & Review).

#### Core Research Deliverables:

1. **50 Real User Daily Pain Points & Breakthrough Software Remediation Matrix**:
   - Deep-dive into the target user persona's daily job (role-playing 8h/day).
   - Document a minimum of **50 distinct pain points** (physical, digital, jurisdictional, visual, data friction) specific to the product domain.
   - Engineer explicit, breakthrough software remediations for all 50 pain points before UI design begins.

2. **Exhaustive Domain Integrations & 3rd-Party Connectors Catalog**:
   - Identify 100% of required domain-specific external integrations, APIs, and tools.
   - *Example (Email Domains)*: Gmail OAuth, IMAP/SMTP dispatchers, Mailgun, SendGrid, SPF/DKIM/DMARC verifiers, Bounce remediation daemons.
   - *Example (Data & Analysis Domains)*: Google Search / Serper APIs, BigQuery, Web Crawlers, OpenStreetMap / Geocoders, Financial APIs, Vector DBs.

3. **Missing Journeys, Sub-Views, Modals & Component Architecture Mapping**:
   - Audit and map all missing 3-step user journeys (*Trigger* ➔ *Feedback* ➔ *Outcome*).
   - Define exact specifications for required sub-views, slide-over right drawers (`<SlideOverDrawer />`), interactive modals, filter bars, and feedback toast notifications.

4. **Top 10 Market Competitor Benchmark & User Alignment Protocol**:
   - Identify and analyze the **top 10 leading apps** in the market closely similar to the project.
   - *Special Rule*: If no direct market competitor exists (novel software concept), the Master Agent MUST pause and trigger an interactive **USER Planning Session** to align product strategy and design intent before proceeding.

---

### 🔬 DEEP RESEARCH GATE 2: Heavy Agentic Upgrades & Market Dominance Strategy (Between Stage 9 & Stage 10)

**Lead Autonomous Agents**: `PERF-01`, `ARCH-01` & `GROWTH-01` (Powered by **DeepSeek-R1 / V3 & Gemini 2.0 Flash**).  
**Execution Timing**: Executed strictly between Stage 9 (Optimization & Tuning) and Stage 10 (System Documentation).

#### Core Research Deliverables:

1. **Heavy Agentic Capability & Tooling Upgrades**:
   - Conduct deep research on advanced agentic capabilities, autonomous subagent tools, custom prompt engineering pipelines, and self-healing algorithms for the specific domain.

2. **Top Competitor Gap Research & Feature Disruption Matrix**:
   - Analyze feature gaps, speed bottlenecks, and UX limitations of top market competitors identified in Gate 1.
   - Benchmark application performance, AI intelligence, and feature depth against market leaders.

3. **Mandatory Market-Beating Goal & Iterative Research Loop**:
   - Establish an explicit, measurable **Market-Beating Goal**: The application MUST demonstrably outperform top market competitors in speed, AI capability, feature depth, and 0% user friction.
   - *Iterative Research Rule*: The Deep Research team MUST conduct continuous research iterations and feature upgrades until the Market-Beating Goal is 100% achieved and attested by the CTO Agent.
