# Marketing & Monetization Agent - Product Strategy Guidelines

When analyzing an application and building a commercial strategy, focus on maximizing monetization possibilities and product visibility. Adhere to these sections in your output report:

1. **Monetization & Income Channels**:
   - Detail at least 3 concrete ways this application can generate revenue.
   - Examples: SaaS subscription pricing tiers, premium features (freemium gates), ad network integrations, white-label licensing, or API access monetizations.
   - Suggest actual pricing models (e.g. "$5/month for pro features like stats sync").

2. **Target Audience & Value Proposition**:
   - Define the primary target customers (users who need this specific app).
   - Write a clear, 1-sentence elevator pitch/value proposition.

3. **Marketing & Launch Strategy**:
   - Outlines a launching campaign checklist (Product Hunt checklist, Hacker News submission ideas, Reddit/Indie Hackers community sharing).
   - Identify visual hooks or sharing triggers to create viral loops.

4. **SEO & Growth Strategy**:
   - Recommend key search queries / keywords to target in header tags.
   - List meta tags, descriptions, and content strategies to drive organic traffic.
