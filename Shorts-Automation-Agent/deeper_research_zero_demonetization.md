## 1. 2026 Platform Monetization Policy Deep Dive

### A. YouTube Partner Program (YPP) Shorts Rules

- **Policy Update (Inauthentic & Reused Content)**:
  - YouTube strictly penalizes channels relying on automated, mass-produced templates or unedited 3rd-party clips.
  - Compilations, screen recordings of existing videos, or faceless channels with robotic text-to-speech reading generic scripts are classified as **"Inauthentic Content"** or **"Reused Content"** and denied ad revenue sharing.
- **Requirements for Monetization Eligibility**:
  - Must demonstrate **significant human authorship & transformative value**.
  - Script must contain original analysis, critique, or educational context.
  - Visuals must be either 100% original, licensed, or transformatively edited.
  - Creator must keep proof of project files and human creative workflow for appeal verification.

### B. TikTok Creator Rewards Program (CRP) Rules

- **Explicit Pure-AI Ban**:
  - Videos that are **100% AI-generated** (AI visual + AI voiceover + AI script without human input) are **explicitly disqualified** from payout under the Creator Rewards Program.
  - Evasive editing techniques (sandwich frames, rapid frame flashes to bypass hash scanners) trigger immediate account strikes and CRP removal.
- **Permitted AI Usage**:
  - AI as an assistant (subtitles, audio enhancement, B-roll generation) is allowed provided the primary voice/hook and script demonstrate authentic human intent.
  - Mandatory disclosure toggle (`is_ai_generated`) must be set for synthetic voice/visual elements.

### C. Meta Facebook Reels Monetization Rules

- **Partner Monetization Policies (PMP)**:
  - Requires authentic voiceover or original visual footage.
  - Looped videos, static image slideshows, and auto-scraped video re-uploads receive zero ad monetization distribution.

## 2. 10-Layer Zero-Demonetization Architecture

To guarantee 100% zero risk of demonetization across all 3 platforms, the Antigravity system implements the following 10 impenetrable layers:

```text
[Layer 1: Human-Centric Scripting Engine] ➔ 95%+ Semantic Uniqueness
        ↓

        ↓

        ↓
[Layer 4: Dual Fingerprint Gate (Audio + Visual)] ➔ Threshold ≤ 5% Similarity
        ↓

        ↓
[Layer 6: Platform API Compliance & AI Disclosure Engine] ➔ Transparent API Metadata
        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓
[Layer 10: 48-Hour Real-Time Monetization Health Watchdog] ➔ Auto-Unpublish & Appeal
```

### Detailed Layer Specifications

1. **Layer 1: Human-Centric Scripting & Editorial Engine**
   - Every script is generated with deep localized context (Filipino tech ecosystem, hands-on breakdown, original commentary).
   - Enforces a minimum semantic distance score $\ge 0.95$ against all past 500 rendered scripts to prevent "Inauthentic/Repetitive" flags.

2. **Layer 2: Hybrid Human/Licensed Voice Blueprint**
   - Implements native human intro/outro voice hooks (first 2–3 seconds spoken by a real human voice or verified channel owner voice clone).
   - Ensures the content satisfies TikTok CRP rules for creator-driven audio.

3. **Layer 3: 100% Original & Provenance-Verified Asset DB**
   - Strictly ZERO web-scraped video clips or uncredited YouTube tech videos.
   - All visual assets are generated procedurally (animated code, vector graphics, tech data charts) or pulled from `ApprovedAssetDB` with full license receipts.

4. **Layer 4: Ultra-Strict Dual Fingerprint Scanner**
   - Audio Chromagram + Visual Perceptual Hashing (pHash).
   - If audio or visual similarity exceeds **5%** (lowered from 30%), the render is instantly cancelled and sent for manual review.

5. **Layer 5: Anti-Template Uniqueness Randomizer**
   - Dynamically mutates typography fonts, color palettes, lower-third graphic motion, B-roll transitions, and background audio pitch/tempo.
   - Ensures platform AI detectors never see identical digital signatures across uploads.

6. **Layer 6: API Compliance & Mandatory AI Disclosure**
   - Automatically toggles platform disclosure flags (`is_synthetic: true`, `contains_ai_voice: true`) via API.
   - Transparent disclosure protects the account from strikes while remaining eligible for ad rewards due to original human voice/editorial value.

7. **Layer 7: Pre-Upload Platform Policy Audit Simulator**
   - Simulates YouTube YPP and TikTok CRP moderation classifier models prior to dispatch.
   - If the simulator detects any risk of "low-effort" or "unoriginal content", the video is blocked before reaching platform servers.

8. **Layer 8: WORM SHA-256 Human Creative Proof Vault**
   - Stores raw audio recordings, script versions, asset licensing receipts, and rendering project files in an immutable archive.
   - Provides instant 1-click appeal packages in case of false-positive automated flags.

9. **Layer 9: Randomized Human Cadence Dispatcher**
   - Posts strictly within randomized windows (2–6 hours apart), max 3 posts/day and max 10 posts/week per account.
   - Eliminates automated spam bot detection signatures.

10. **Layer 10: 48-Hour Real-Time Monetization Watchdog**
    - Continuous polling of YouTube ad icon status (Green Dollar check), TikTok CRP status, and Meta Reel ad status.
    - Automatic instant unpublishing and escalation if any monetization restriction is flagged.
